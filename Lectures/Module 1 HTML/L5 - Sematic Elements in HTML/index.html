Facts About Dogs

Dogs have a sense of time. It's been proven that they know the difference between one hour and five. If conditionedto, they can predict future events such as regular walk times.

A study was conducted on dogs being away from their owners for various hours and the studies show that dogs who were away from their owners the longest showed the greatest amount of affection!
