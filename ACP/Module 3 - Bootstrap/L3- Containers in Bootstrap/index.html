Hobbies

About Hobbies:

Hobbies are activities pursued for enjoyment, typically during leisure time. They offer a wide range of benefits, including stress relief, skill development, and social interaction. Choosing a hobby often depends on personal interests and available resources. Hobbies can be creative, like painting or writing, or active, like sports or hiking. They provide a healthy escape from daily routines and contribute to overall well-being.
